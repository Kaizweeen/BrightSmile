# Bright Smile Dental Clinic: 40-second promo

- **Format:** 9:16 vertical, 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, 40 s. There is also a 16:9 landscape cut (1920 × 1080) with the same scenes, words and soundtrack.
- **Made for:** TikTok, Facebook Reels and YouTube Shorts (9:16). The same file works on all three. Use the 16:9 cut for YouTube, Facebook feed posts, the website and screens in the clinic.
- **Audience:** families and adults in Makati who are scrolling on their phones. Many watch with the sound off, so captions carry the voiceover.
- **Look:** brand teal `#0d8781`, deep navy-teal `#0b3d4a`, logo cream `#fdf5ef`, Figtree type, the tooth emblem. Smooth iris, wipe and teal-sweep transitions, with text that pops and slides in. Every scene pushes in slowly (about 6%), so no frame ever stands still.
- **Files:** `brightsmile-promo-9x16.mp4` and `brightsmile-promo-16x9.mp4` (finished: picture, voiceover, sound effects and music), `promo-picture.mp4` and `promo-picture-16x9.mp4` (picture only), `promo.html` and `promo-16x9.html` (animation source), `render.mjs` (renders the picture), `audio.py` (makes the voiceover, effects and music and mixes them on)

## Selling points, and what was left out

The video uses only facts from `PRODUCT.md` and the landing page:

1. **Easy booking:** book online in a minute, and the clinic confirms by text message.
2. **Gentle care for the whole family:** two dentists treat kids and adults. Services run from cleaning, *pasta* and *bunot* to braces and root canal.
3. **Near you:** three clinics in Makati with the same hours at all three, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

**Call to action:** "Book your visit today" with brightsmile.pro, 0956-342-1673, and the booking QR code from the landing page.

These were left out on purpose: "painless", "affordable packages" and "free consultation". `PRODUCT.md` says no prices or claims may be shown that the clinic hasn't confirmed. "Painless" is also a medical claim. If the clinic does offer a free consultation, change the CTA headline in `promo.html` to "Book your FREE consultation", change the last voiceover line to match, and render again.

## Voiceover script (about 75 words)

Read it warm and upbeat with a slight smile, like a friendly front-desk staffer. Speak at a natural pace and leave short pauses at the breaks below. A Filipino-English (Taglish-friendly) accent fits the audience best.

> **[0:00]** When was your last dental check-up?
> **[0:03]** At Bright Smile in Makati, a healthy smile is easy.
> **[0:07]** Book online in a minute. No calls needed. *(beat)* Pick a time, and we'll text to confirm your visit.
> **[0:15]** Gentle, friendly care for kids and adults, from cleaning and *pasta* to braces and root canal.
> **[0:24]** Three clinics in Makati, open nine to five. *(beat)* There's one near you.
> **[0:32]** Book your visit today at brightsmile dot pro. *(beat)* Your bright smile starts here.

**Optional Tagalog hook:** open with "Kailan ka huling nagpa-check-up?" and keep the rest the same. The Tagalog line is already on screen.

## Music brief

- **Mood:** bright, feel-good, modern. Light pop or tropical-house with a clean beat, about 110–120 BPM.
- **Instruments:** plucked synths or ukulele, soft claps, airy pads, a light marimba or bell motif. Avoid heavy bass drops and vocals, which would compete with the voiceover.
- **Shape:** start right on beat one so the hook lands. Keep the music under the voiceover at about −18 dB to −20 dB, and let it lift at 0:32 for the call to action. Finish on a clean button ending or let it ring out at 0:40.
- **Accents:** a soft "ding" when the check badge lands (0:12), light pops on the chips (0:17–0:20) and the pins (0:25–0:27), and a whoosh on each transition (0:03, 0:15, 0:24, 0:32).
- **Where to get it:** TikTok's and Facebook's own commercial music libraries, YouTube Audio Library, or a licensed stock track. Search terms: "upbeat corporate", "happy ukulele", "fresh tropical pop". A business account must use commercially licensed music.

## Sound in the finished video

**Voiceover.** The voice is Kokoro's `af_heart`, a warm American English voice, generated locally by `audio.py`. Two words are pronounced as they are in Makati: "Makati" with a real *t*, and "brightsmile" as two words. Each line starts with its caption:

To use a real voice artist instead, record the lines to these times and replace `voiceover.wav` with the recording. Then run only the mix step (`main()` in `audio.py`) or mix in CapCut.

**Sound effects.** The effects are synthesised in `audio.py`, so there is nothing to license:
- soft ticks as the hook words land, and a bouncy pop for the "?"
- whooshes on every transition (0:03, 0:07, 0:15, 0:24, 0:32)
- a pop and sparkle for the logo
- screen taps on the time slot and the button, a two-note text-message chime, and a "confirmed" arpeggio on the check badge
- bubbly pops rising in pitch for the service chips
- water-drop plips as the map pins land, and clock ticks
- a rising swell into the URL, and a bright closing chime with a sparkle on "Your bright smile starts here"

The effects duck under the voice. The mix is normalised to −14 LUFS, the level TikTok, Reels and YouTube play at.

**Music.** An original backing track is synthesised in `audio.py`, so it is free to use with no licence. It is bright C-major pop at about 116 BPM, built from:
- a ukulele-like plucked arpeggio
- a warm, wide pad
- a bouncy bass
- a light four-on-the-floor kick, claps and shakers
- a marimba-bell hook from 0:24

The tempo puts bar lines on the scene changes:
- **0:00–0:03:** the intro swells in and a riser lifts into the beat drop at the logo (0:03).
- **0:07, 0:15 and 0:24:** new bars land on the scene changes, with a clap roll into the map wipe.
- **0:31:** the kick drops out and a riser builds into the call to action.
- **0:32:** the call to action lifts, with the bells louder and open hi-hats.
- **0:38.2:** the track ends on one big ringing chord that fades out by 0:40.

The music pumps gently with the kick, ducks under the voice, and comes up in the gaps between lines.

To use a licensed track instead, replace `music.wav` with it (40 s, 48 kHz) and run the mix step again. You can also mix it on in the TikTok or CapCut editor at about 15–20% volume.

## Posting tips

- **TikTok / Reels:** post it natively; don't share it as a YouTube link. Use the video's own captions and turn auto-captions off so the text doesn't double. Choose 0:33 (the call-to-action screen) as the cover frame. Suggested caption: *"Kailan ka huling nagpa-check-up? 🦷✨ Book online in a minute at brightsmile.pro. 3 clinics in Makati, open 9 AM–5 PM. #MakatiDentist #DentalClinicMakati #BrightSmile"*
- **YouTube Shorts:** keep it under 60 s (it is 40 s) and put brightsmile.pro in the description.
- **Facebook / YouTube feed (16:9):** post `brightsmile-promo-16x9.mp4`. Each scene puts the headline on the left and the visual on the right, and the captions run along the bottom. Choose 0:36 as the YouTube thumbnail, or design one with the hook line.

## Changing and rendering

Every motion in `promo.html` is one CSS animation, set with `data-a="name start duration"` (entrance) or `data-o="…"` (exit). Times are in seconds from the start of the video. To preview the animation, open `promo.html` in Chrome; to check a single moment, add `?t=12.5` to the URL. To render:

```bash
npm install                                          # once, from the repo root
node marketing/promo-video/render.mjs --stills 2 10 34   # PNGs of single moments
node marketing/promo-video/render.mjs                # promo-picture.mp4, about 5 minutes
python marketing/promo-video/audio.py                # voiceover + effects + music -> brightsmile-promo-9x16.mp4
node marketing/promo-video/render.mjs --wide         # promo-picture-16x9.mp4 (add --stills to check moments)
```

`promo-16x9.html` uses the same `data-a` times as `promo.html`. When you change a time or a line in one, change the other to match.

If you change only the picture and keep every time the same, you don't need the voice models. Copy the finished soundtrack onto the new picture instead:

```bash
cd marketing/promo-video
ffmpeg -i promo-picture.mp4 -i brightsmile-promo-9x16.mp4 -map 0:v -map 1:a -c copy -movflags +faststart new.mp4 && mv new.mp4 brightsmile-promo-9x16.mp4
# the 16:9 cut takes the same soundtrack:
ffmpeg -i promo-picture-16x9.mp4 -i brightsmile-promo-9x16.mp4 -map 0:v -map 1:a -c copy -movflags +faststart brightsmile-promo-16x9.mp4
```

`audio.py` needs `pip install kokoro-onnx==0.4.9 soundfile scipy`. It also needs `kokoro-v1.0.onnx` and `voices-v1.0.bin` from [kokoro-onnx's model-files-v1.0 release](https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx/releases/tag/model-files-v1.0) in `marketing/promo-video/models/` (git ignores that folder), or set `KOKORO_DIR` to wherever they are. To change a voice line, edit `LINES` in `audio.py` and keep it inside its caption's time in `promo.html`.

You need ffmpeg and a Chromium: `npx playwright install chromium`, or set `PLAYWRIGHT_CHANNEL=chrome` or `CHROMIUM_PATH`. The QR code on the CTA screen is drawn from the same rows as the landing page (`src/app/clinic/data.ts`), so it opens the real booking page. If the booking link changes, update the rows in both places.
